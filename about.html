Atelier Manifesto

The Three Sacred Tenets of Our Craft

In an era of mass automation, our studio adheres to three non-negotiable tenets.

Tenet I • Tooling Truth

Zero Automat CNC Milling

Every curve and hollow is carved by hand with Sheffield steel. Computers cannot feel grain.

Tooling: 140+ Registered Gouges & Knives
Human Authorship Certified
Tenet II • Forestry Sanctity

Windfall Salvage Exclusively

Never felling living trees. Sourced exclusively from windfalls, architectural salvage, bog oak.

Provenance: 100% Chain-of-Custody Verified
FSC Certified Sourcing
