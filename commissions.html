Atelier Methodology

The Five Stages of a Master Commission

Structured progression from initial sketches to white-glove rigging.

01

Spatial Consultation & Site Review

Reviewing elevations and lighting angles to establish precise scale.

Week 1–2 • Site Survey
02

Charcoal Cartoon & Clay Maquette

Hand-drawn 1:1 charcoal cartoons and bench maquettes for approval.

Week 3–4 • Maquette Approval
03

Monolithic Timber Block Selection

Selecting seasoned English Oak, Claro Walnut, or 5,000-year Bog Oak.

Week 5–6 • Heartwood Sourcing
04

Progressive Chisel Articulation

Hand-carved over 6 to 20 weeks with bi-weekly studio photo reports.

6–20 Weeks • Hand Carving
05

White-Glove Installation & Hallmark
