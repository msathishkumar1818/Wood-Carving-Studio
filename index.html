The Guild Methodology

Four Rhythms of Timber Transformation

Four disciplined stages of uncompromised hand carving from log to polish.

01 Phase One

Old-Growth Timber Selection

Seasoned timber dried 12+ years to eliminate tension and prevent cracking.

Check: Moisture < 9% Stability
02 Phase Two

Adze & Mallet Rough Hewing

Establishing primal volumes using Japanese adzes and beechwood mallets.

Volume: Block Geometric Massing
03 Phase Three

Micro-Chisel Undercutting

Refining intricate botanical relief with Swiss gouges down to 0.5mm.

Tolerances: Hand Sub-Millimeter
04 Phase Four

Organic Beeswax Finishing

Hand-rubbed cold-pressed walnut oil and pure local beeswax seal.

Protection: 100% Breathable Luster
